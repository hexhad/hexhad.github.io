NotchPal

Your Claude Code sessions, living in the notch.

An animated hexagon shows whether Claude is thinking, working or waiting, and one click jumps to that session. The Android companion approves permission requests remotely.

Website Google Play
Android

BarQR

Make a QR code in seconds.

Custom colours and icons, seven formats each test-scanned for readability, and multi-code detection from the camera or any photo. No accounts, ads or analytics.

Website Google Play
macOS

Droid Transfer

Your Android phone, one drag away.

A Finder-style file browser over USB or Wi-Fi, with screen mirroring, drag-and-drop APK installs and no file size limit.

Website
macOS

MirrorMe

A camera for your simulators.

Drag an iOS Simulator or Android emulator over a QR code and its camera sees it live.

How HEXHAD builds.

The hexagon is the idea: small cells that are complete on their own and fit together.

One job per app.

Each product solves one problem from daily mobile work, then gets out of the way.

Your device, your data.

Where an app can work locally and without an account, it does.

Reusable parts go public.

When a piece is useful beyond one app, it ships as an open-source package.
